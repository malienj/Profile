About Manavi

I am a cosmochemist, independent researcher and expedition planner. One question runs through all my work: how do complex systems, whether ancient stars, cave microbes or human beings, form, adapt and endure under extreme conditions?

From stellar furnaces to the deep underground

My path began in laboratory astrophysics and cosmochemistry, reading the isotopic fingerprints of presolar stardust to understand the nuclear furnaces of dying stars. Along the way I spent a season on the Antarctic ice searching for meteorites.

Over time that curiosity widened from deep space to extreme environments on Earth. Today my work spans stellar nucleosynthesis, cave and karst exploration, space health and grassroots education, linking planetary science with human resilience, fieldwork and community.
