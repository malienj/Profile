Space health and human resilience

Living beyond Earth means understanding how the body handles microgravity and radiation, and how the mind copes with long isolation. The same questions about endurance under pressure apply on the ground, in labs and lecture halls.

Mental health in academia

Research careers come with real pressure: deadlines, uncertainty, isolation, and the expectation to keep going regardless. I bring certified Mental Health First Aid (MHFA) training into higher education, and work to make burnout and stress easier to talk about.

What I offer

  • Workshops for undergraduate and graduate researchers
  • Practical frameworks for managing stress and burnout
  • Wellness consulting for departments and universities
