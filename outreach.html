Outreach and community

Science belongs to everyone, and it does the most good when it starts in the community. I bring hands-on science to rural and tribal schools, and speak to students, universities and the public about the Universe and how we explore it.

Science in tribal and rural schools

I design and run STEM programmes for tribal villages and under-served rural schools, built around inquiry-based learning, local relevance, and hands-on science kits that teachers can keep using.

Talks and public speaking

I speak to schools, universities and general audiences about planetary science, cosmochemistry, space health and non-linear paths in research. Topics include:

  • Stardust in our hands: what meteorites reveal about the Universe
  • Exploring the underworld: caves as portals to other planets
  • Living in the void: the frontier of space health
  • Resilience in the field and the lab: mental health in academia
